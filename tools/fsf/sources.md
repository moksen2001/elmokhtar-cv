- Wikipedia "Senegal national football team" (raw, fetched 2 Oct 2026) + "2026 FIFA World Cup Group I":
  - 2026 FIFA World Cup, Group I: 16 Jun France 3-1 Sénégal (MetLife Stadium; Mbaye 90+5) · 22 Jun Norvège 3-2 Sénégal (MetLife; I. Senegal 3rd, qualified as a best third.
  - Round of 32, 1 Jul 2026: Belgique 3-2 Sénégal a.p. (Senegal led 2-0 until the 85th minute; late Tielemans penalty).
  - Coaches: Pape Thiaw 2024–2026; **Patrick Vieira appointed 18 Aug 2026** (Eurosport https://www.eurosport.fr/football/le-francais-patrick-vieira-nomme-selectionneur-du-senegal-officiel_sto23329193/story.shtml ; foot-africa: 4-year contract).
  - Captain Kalidou Koulibaly; most caps Idrissa Gueye (136); top scorer Sadio Mané (55). Home stadium: Diamniadio Olympic Stadium (Stade Abdoulaye Wade).
  - AFCON 2025 final, 18 Jan 2026, Rabat (Prince Moulay Abdellah Stadium): Sénégal 1-0 Maroc a.p. (Pape Gueye 94'). On 17 Mar 2026 the CAF Appeal Board ruled a forfeit (players' walk-off) and awarded the title to Morocco (BBC https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/ce949glzzglo). FSF appealed to CAS; **CAS hearing set for 8 Oct 2026** (Xalima 30 Sep 2026 https://xalimasn.com/2026/09/30/senegal-maroc-le-tas-fixe-au-8-octobre-laudience-sur-la-finale-de-la-can-2025/ ; Pulse.sn). → The app states this neutrally (title "en litige devant le TAS"), never "double champion".
  - AFCON 2021 (Cameroon) champions — 1st title.

## 6. Stadiums
- Stade Abdoulaye Wade (Diamniadio): opened Feb 2022, capacity 50,000, Tabanlıoğlu Architects, built by Summa in 18 months, cost 156 bn FCFA, pitch 105×68 m, CAF 4-star; host of YOG Dakar 2026 ceremonies (Wikipedia "Abdoulaye Wade Stadium").
- Stade Léopold Sédar Senghor (Dakar, ex "Stade de l'Amitié"): opened 31 Oct 1985, capacity 40,000, renovated 2022–2025, record 75,000 (SEN–NGA 12 Jan 1992), hosted the 1992 AFCON final (Wikipedia "Leopold Sédar Senghor Stadium").
