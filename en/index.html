Method

Three concrete cases

The same thread every time: understand the problem with the people who live it, ship something usable, measure what changed.

Trade Finance · 2025 – 26

Recurring documents filled in by hand in the Middle Office

1The problem

Middle Office teams filled in recurring documents such as Notices of Assignment by hand: slow, repetitive work prone to data-entry errors.

2What I did

I designed Copilot prompts that pre-fill these documents from the deal information, tested them with users, then rolled them out to the teams.

3The result

Documents such as Notices of Assignment completed much faster, with prompts adopted by Middle Office teams in Paris, Geneva and Bangalore.

IT Dept · Case Management · 2025

An internal portal to rethink with the people who use it

1The problem

The Case Management portal needed to better match the needs of the teams who use it every day.

2What I did

Needs analysis, participatory workshops to co-build the content, 10 screens prototyped in Figma, then development follow-up in a 14-person Scrum team on PEGA.

3The result

A portal redesigned with its users, plus an automated Jira form that centralizes requests and shortens ticket creation.
